2. Privacy policy

Short version: the app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers that receive your media. Your videos stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Media you choose and your project data are saved in your browser’s local storage (IndexedDB) on your device. They are never uploaded to the developer. Exporting happens on your device. The app only reads files you pick yourself.

What the app does not collect

The developer does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, advertising ID or usage statistics.

Third-party services that see your IP address

To start, the app downloads code and fonts from public content delivery networks: cdnjs.cloudflare.com, unpkg.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net, fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. The web version is also hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers can see your IP address and basic device information when your device requests files, under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Clear the site data for this app in your browser, or uninstall the Android app, to remove saved projects and media.

Children

The app is not directed at children. A parent or guardian should supervise use by minors.
